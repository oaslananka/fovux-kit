# Contributor Ladder and Maintainer Expectations

## Contributor

Opens focused issues or PRs with validation evidence and follows local-first, no-telemetry, and safety policies.

## Regular contributor

Handles scoped issues end-to-end, updates docs/tests, and responds to review.

## Maintainer

Reviews high-risk changes, release workflows, security-sensitive code, labels, milestones, and ADRs.

## Agent contributor

States validation performed, avoids broad destructive changes, respects issue scope, and closes issues only after verification.
